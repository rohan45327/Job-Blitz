# JobBlitz — Hackathon Demo Storyboard & Pitch Guide

## 1. Hackathon Narrative & Differentiator

> **"Most job platforms help candidates FIND jobs. Job Blitz helps candidates UNDERSTAND, PREPARE FOR, and ACT ON the right jobs."**

Job Blitz turns every job posting into an actionable **Personal Career Mission**.

---

## 2. 3-to-5 Minute Polished Demo Storyboard

```
0:00 - 0:20 | DAILY COMMAND CENTER
- Open Job Blitz on iQOO Phone.
- Show Daily Command Center: "Today's Action Plan: 3 High-Priority Jobs, 1 Mock Interview, 2 Follow-ups".

0:20 - 0:50 | DUAL SCORING & EVIDENCE PANEL
- Tap "Senior AI Engineer — Paytm".
- Point out Dual Badges: 94% MATCH (Cyan) vs 72% READY (Emerald).
- Tap "Why 72% Ready?" -> Opens Evidence Panel Modal with [OFFICIAL] and [PUBLIC SIGNAL] pills.

0:50 - 1:20 | CAMERA JOB CAPTURE (Phone-Native Feature)
- Point phone camera at a printed hiring flyer / poster.
- Instant on-device OCR extracts Company, Title, and Skills via Snapdragon NPU.
- Automatically generates job workspace.

1:20 - 2:00 | VOICE MOCK INTERVIEW & RESUME DEFENSE
- Tap "Start Mock Interview".
- Speak response to resume defense question: "Explain the architecture of your PyTorch project."
- On-device speech recognition + local AI evaluates STAR structure & technical depth in real-time.

2:00 - 2:30 | OFFICE KIT DEEP WORK BRIDGE
- Toggle "Deep Analysis Mode".
- Office Kit transfers 25-page company dossier to laptop workstation.
- Laptop processes heavy RAG and pushes deep candidate benchmark synthesis back to phone screen in < 2 seconds.

2:30 - 3:00 | APPLICATION QUALITY GATE & TRACKER
- Application Quality Gate verifies: Profile Ready, Resume Variant Tailored, Project Evidence Mapped.
- Tap "Review & Submit" (Explicit user approval).
- Job moves automatically into Application Tracker pipeline with follow-up timer set.
```

## 3. Test Modes for Judges

### 3.1 RED LIGHT TEST (Phone-Only)
- Disconnect laptop and disable internet connection.
- Demonstrate that job browsing, saved jobs, match scoring, local SLM gap analysis, offline preparation roadmap, and voice interview work **100% on-device on the phone**.

### 3.2 GREEN LIGHT TEST (Laptop Connected)
- Connect Office Kit bridge.
- Trigger "Deep Analysis Mode" to showcase heavy multi-page dossier processing offloaded to laptop.
